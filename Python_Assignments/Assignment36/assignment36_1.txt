Why is it a Multiclass Classification Problem?

A binary classification problem has 2 possible output classes.

Examples:

Spam / Not Spam
Cricket / Tennis

The Iris dataset has 3 possible output classes.

Setosa

Since the model chooses one of three classes, it is a multiclass classification problem.
